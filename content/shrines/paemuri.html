Love is all about wanting what is best for the other person. Even if it tears you apart, even if you don’t feel seen. Love is about caring and sharing the good and the bad, showing your true colors without being scared of judgment.

Rafael is my husband, but before that, we were high school sweethearts. We were too shy and naive to say anything to each other, and to even recognize we were in love at all. Years later we gave it a shot, but things didn’t work out. With other things on our minds, we went our separate ways once again.

The year was 2020. The pandemic was at its peak, and Discord was the only way to keep in touch. Amidst the chaos, we tried again.

Success!

I found myself spending more time at his place than at my own, missing the little aspects of living together with his dumbass and shitty morning dances. I missed fighting over bed space and spending lazy nights watching Ghibli movies.

Butterflies and sunny vibes filled our days, I felt like the world could end at any moment and I’d still be happy, warm, and safe.

Silly jokes filled the space between us while we made morning coffee, and talks about deep and controversial matters just because one of us was procrastinating on something. I couldn’t imagine my life without it anymore.

We got married. That’s all we could ask for. Not because of the legal state, it was just a paper, but because we cherished each other.

I realized that love is all about wanting what is best for the other person without expecting anything in return. You know your loved one will be there for you, giving you the love you need to heal. And even if you don’t feel understood, you will feel safe expressing your feelings, and both of you will try to find a way so the relationship doesn’t get heavy for either of you.

Love is about caring and sharing the good and the bad, showing your true colors without being scared of judgment, and most importantly growing together, learning to respect boundaries, and sensitive topics, and being responsible for what you bring to the relationship.

Everything needs maintenance. There’s no such thing as a happily ever after with a perfect couple. We can only accept and love each other for who we truly are, and that’s what we’re learning to do together. Slowly learning how to face whatever life has ahead of us and help each other through it.
