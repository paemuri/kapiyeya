My family has always supported the part of me that wants to save all the animals I come across.
Now, as an adult, I can channel that energy into helping them in ways that make me feel fulfilled. I've received help from an NGO, friends, family members, neighbors, and, most importantly, my husband.
Poor man had to deal with 11 energetic, flea-infested kittens in our bedroom because there was no other room available. Lol. sidebar-right: |
Trap-Neuter-Return
TNR has helped me clear feral-cat hotspots in the neighborhood. With kittens, it's usually best to try to find them homes, and so far, I've been lucky with that.

Adult feral cats, or community cats on the other hand, tend to be much harder to adopt due to a lack of human interaction as kittens.
You can help
Check your local shelters, in most cases you can help by just giving your time.
Or simply notice the animais that live in your neighborhood and see what you can do.
You can always be useful, just do some light research about safety first!
